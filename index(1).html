About Horse Peak Gelatin

Horse Peak Gelatin is a gummy-style dietary supplement presented in a bottle of 30 gummies. Its label focuses on three themes: natural vitality, strength and daily energy.*

What Is Gelatin?

Gelatin is a protein produced by partially breaking down collagen. It dissolves in warm liquid and sets as it cools, which is why it is the base for many gummies and jellies. In supplements, gelatin gives gummies their soft, chewy texture and makes them easy to take every day.
